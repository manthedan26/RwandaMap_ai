Education snapshot

  • 1,576 public schools, 2,083 government-subsidized and 1,337 private
  • 4,822,507 learners, up 1.2% from 4,766,125 in 2023/24
  • 14,104,969 people in Rwanda in 2025 (NISR projection); learners are 34.2% of them
  • 1,991 schools with secondary level and 581 with TVET
  • 57:1 pupils per classroom in primary schools

Source: MINEDUC Education Statistical Yearbook 2024/2025.

Library Official REB books and national exam past papers

Books are published by the Rwanda Education Board (REB) and open on the official REB eLearning site. Past papers open the official NESA folders. This platform links to the official sources and does not copy their files. Some resources may ask for a free REB account.

Data principle

About Manthedan · RwandaMap AI · SchoolMap AI Rwanda

What is RwandaMap AI?

This platform is built by Manthedan to make education information in Rwanda easier to discover, understand and access: official REB books, national exam past papers and school data, with an AI assistant that speaks Kinyarwanda, English and French.

Its school-data application, SchoolMap AI Rwanda, uses public data and AI to show how schools are distributed and where access may need a closer look.
